Privacy Policy

In short: pixelday works in full without an account. Your drawings stay on your device and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud, which we cannot access. Photos you convert never leave your device. Our server only holds what you choose to make public in the community and the small account you create for it. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your personal information.

This policy explains what personal data the pixelday app (iPhone, iPad, its home-screen widget and its iMessage sticker pack) and the pixelday website handle, why, for how long, who else is involved and what rights you have. The English version is the official one; the Spanish translation below is provided for convenience, and if they differ, the English version controls.

1. Who we are

pixelday is made and operated by Guillermo C. Chedraui Sotomayor (known as Charvel Chedraui), an individual developer resident in Ecuador (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller (responsable del tratamiento) for the personal data described here.

Given the small amount and the nature of the data we process, we have not appointed a data protection officer (delegado de protección de datos). If the law requires one in the future, we will name them here. A company we are setting up is expected to take over pixelday; see section 18.

2. Using pixelday without an account

Drawing your daily pixel, the calendar, the Studio, converting photos to pixel art, stickers, the widget, reminders, backups and saving to Photos all work without an account. In that case, pixelday sends us no personal data at all, except crash reports (section 4.10). The following stays on your device and never reaches us:

  • Your drawings, calendar, Studio pieces, settings, streaks, medals and statistics. They are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The widget and the sticker pack read them from a container that is shared only between the pixelday app and its own extensions on the same device.
  • Photos you convert. The photo you pick or take is turned into pixel art on the device itself. The original photo is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • iCloud sync (optional). If you turn on iCloud sync, your drawings are also stored as a file in pixelday's container in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. Apple provides that storage to you under its own terms and privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Backup files. “Save a copy” creates a backup file that you decide where to keep. We never receive it.
  • Daily reminders. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. We do not operate a push notification server and do not receive a push token.

3. The optional account and community

The community (the feed of shared daily pixels and public profiles) is optional. To use it you sign in with Sign in with Apple, the only sign-in method. When you sign in, the app does not ask Apple for your name or your email address, so Apple does not give them to us. We never receive your Apple password. Our server checks the sign-in token and keeps only Apple's anonymous, app-specific user identifier. If an Apple token ever contained an email address, we would not read or store it.

Everything you make public (your public name, profile photo, cover, profile numbers, follower and following counts and shared drawings) can be seen by other people who use the pixelday community. Others may take screenshots or otherwise copy what they see; removing your content from pixelday cannot recall such copies. Share only what you are comfortable making public.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos (selected photos): to convert a photo you pick into pixel art, or to choose a profile photo or cover. Only the photos you select are accessed.
  • Photos (add only): to save drawings and stickers to your library when you ask.
  • Camera: to take a photo and convert it into pixel art, on the device.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, rent it or give it to advertisers or data brokers. We disclose it only to:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which hosts our server and database and processes request data on our behalf.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) (United States), which processes crash reports on our behalf.
  • Apple, which provides Sign in with Apple, iCloud (directly to you), the App Store and in-app payments under its own privacy policy. When you delete your account we ask Apple to revoke pixelday's access to your Sign in with Apple.
  • Authorities and organizations when the law requires it, for example a valid court order, or reports of child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and competent authorities; and when necessary to protect people's safety or our legal rights.
  • A successor, such as the company that is expected to take over pixelday (section 18), which will remain bound by this policy.

Our service providers may use the data only to provide their services to us, under contracts (data processing agreements) that require protection equivalent to this policy.

7. No selling, no sharing for ads, no tracking

We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA) defines those terms, and have not done so in the last 12 months. We do not use personal data for targeted advertising or profiling. pixelday does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, so it does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and never accesses the advertising identifier. There are no ads and no analytics SDKs in the app. Because we do not sell or share personal data, there is nothing to opt out of; we nevertheless honor browser opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control as a request not to sell or share.

8. International transfers

We are based in Ecuador. Our server and database are hosted by Cloudflare in the United States (with Cloudflare's global network delivering requests), and crash reports are processed by Sentry in the United States. Your data is therefore transferred to and processed in the United States and possibly other countries where these providers operate, whose laws may differ from yours. We rely on the safeguards these providers offer: their data processing agreements, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and their self-certification to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (including the UK and Swiss extensions). For transfers from Ecuador, we use providers that act on our behalf under contracts with appropriate guarantees, as the Ecuadorian Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP) and the rules of the Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales (SPDP) provide. You may ask us for more information about these safeguards.

10. Deleting your account and data

  1. Open pixelday and go to Settings > Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

We first ask Apple to revoke pixelday's Sign in with Apple access, then permanently delete from our server your account, public name, profile photo, cover, profile numbers, every shared drawing, your follows, blocks, the reports you made and the reports about your content, your sessions and the Apple refresh token. Copies in our database backups disappear within 30 days. The only exception is the ban hash described in 4.11 for suspended accounts. Your drawings on your device, in your iCloud and in your backup files stay with you; delete them by deleting the app, turning off iCloud sync and removing pixelday's data in your iCloud settings, and deleting your backup files.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete what you published. If you cannot use the app, email us from any address with your public name and we will delete the account after verifying it; see Delete account. To remove a single drawing, open its menu in the feed and choose “Remove from the feed”.

11. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account or use the community, and older if your country requires it to consent on your own to the processing of your data (in some EU countries up to 16); below that age, you need permission from a parent or guardian. Using pixelday without an account sends us no personal data from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, or to a child below the required age without parental permission, we will delete the account and its data. Parents or guardians who believe this has happened can write to charvel.chedraui@gmail.com.

12. Security

We collect as little as possible and protect what we hold with reasonable technical and organizational measures, including: encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic; no passwords (sign-in is delegated to Apple); session tokens stored in the device's Keychain and kept only as one-way hashes on our server; checks on every request that you can only change your own data; rate limits against abuse; admin access limited to the operator and protected by a secret credential; metadata stripped from uploaded photos; and crash reporting configured to exclude personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a security incident affects your personal data, we will notify you and the competent authorities where the law requires it. To report a vulnerability, see our security page.

13. Artificial intelligence

pixelday has no AI features. Photo-to-pixel-art conversion is a deterministic algorithm that runs on your device. We do not send your data to any AI provider or use it to train AI models.

14. Our website and cookies

The pages load a font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. The website sets no cookies, has no analytics and no advertising. We do not track visitors over time or across other sites, and we treat Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals as described in section 7.

  • Tus dibujos, calendario, piezas del Estudio, ajustes, rachas, medallas y estadísticas. Se guardan en el almacenamiento privado de la app en tu dispositivo. El widget y el paquete de stickers los leen de un contenedor compartido solo entre la app pixelday y sus propias extensiones en el mismo dispositivo.
  • Las fotos que conviertes. La foto que eliges o tomas se convierte en pixel art en el propio dispositivo. La foto original nunca se sube a nuestro servidor ni a nadie más.
  • Sincronización con iCloud (opcional). Si activas la sincronización con iCloud, tus dibujos también se guardan como un archivo en el contenedor de pixelday dentro de tu propio iCloud Drive, bajo tu cuenta de Apple. Apple te ofrece ese almacenamiento según sus propios términos y política de privacidad; nosotros no tenemos acceso a él.
  • Archivos de copia de seguridad. «Guardar una copia» crea un archivo que tú decides dónde guardar. Nunca lo recibimos.
  • Recordatorios diarios. Son notificaciones locales programadas en el dispositivo. No operamos un servidor de notificaciones push ni recibimos un token push.

3. La cuenta y la comunidad opcionales

La comunidad (el feed de pixeles del día compartidos y los perfiles públicos) es opcional. Para usarla inicias sesión con Iniciar sesión con Apple, el único método de acceso. Al iniciar sesión, la app no le pide a Apple tu nombre ni tu correo electrónico, así que Apple no nos los entrega. Nunca recibimos tu contraseña de Apple. Nuestro servidor verifica el token de inicio de sesión y guarda solo el identificador de usuario anónimo que Apple genera para esta app. Si un token de Apple llegara a contener un correo electrónico, no lo leeríamos ni lo guardaríamos.

Todo lo que haces público (tu nombre público, foto de perfil, portada, números del perfil, número de seguidores y seguidos, y dibujos compartidos) puede ser visto por otras personas que usan la comunidad de pixelday. Otras personas pueden hacer capturas de pantalla o copiar lo que ven; quitar tu contenido de pixelday no puede recuperar esas copias. Comparte solo lo que te parezca bien hacer público.

No vendemos datos personales, no los alquilamos ni se los damos a anunciantes o intermediarios de datos. Solo los comunicamos a:

  • Otros usuarios, en el caso del contenido que haces público (sección 3).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Estados Unidos), que aloja nuestro servidor y base de datos y trata los datos de las solicitudes por cuenta nuestra.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) (Estados Unidos), que trata los informes de fallos por cuenta nuestra.
  • Apple, que presta Iniciar sesión con Apple, iCloud (directamente a ti), el App Store y los pagos dentro de la app según su propia política de privacidad. Cuando eliminas tu cuenta, pedimos a Apple que revoque el acceso de pixelday a tu Iniciar sesión con Apple.

Nuestros proveedores solo pueden usar los datos para prestarnos sus servicios, bajo contratos (acuerdos de tratamiento de datos) que exigen una protección equivalente a esta política.

7. No vendemos, no compartimos para publicidad, no rastreamos

No «vendemos» ni «compartimos» información personal tal como definen esos términos la Ley de Privacidad del Consumidor de California (CCPA, modificada por la CPRA), y no lo hemos hecho en los últimos 12 meses. No usamos datos personales para publicidad dirigida ni para elaborar perfiles. pixelday no te rastrea en apps ni sitios web de otras empresas, por eso no muestra el aviso de Transparencia de Rastreo de Apple y nunca accede al identificador de publicidad. La app no tiene anuncios ni SDK de analítica. Como no vendemos ni compartimos datos personales, no hay nada de lo que excluirse; aun así, respetamos señales del navegador como Global Privacy Control como una solicitud de no vender ni compartir.

12. Seguridad

Recopilamos lo mínimo y protegemos lo que guardamos con medidas técnicas y organizativas razonables, entre ellas: conexiones cifradas (HTTPS) para todo el tráfico; sin contraseñas (el inicio de sesión lo gestiona Apple); tokens de sesión guardados en el Llavero del dispositivo y solo como huellas irreversibles en nuestro servidor; comprobaciones en cada solicitud para que solo puedas cambiar tus propios datos; límites de uso contra abusos; acceso de administración limitado al operador y protegido con una credencial secreta; eliminación de metadatos de las fotos subidas; e informes de fallos configurados para excluir datos personales. Ningún método de transmisión o almacenamiento es totalmente seguro. Si un incidente de seguridad afecta a tus datos personales, te lo notificaremos a ti y a las autoridades competentes cuando la ley lo exija. Para reportar una vulnerabilidad, consulta nuestra página de seguridad.

13. Inteligencia artificial

pixelday no tiene funciones de IA. La conversión de fotos en pixel art es un algoritmo determinista que se ejecuta en tu dispositivo. No enviamos tus datos a ningún proveedor de IA ni los usamos para entrenar modelos de IA.
